# Privacy Policy — Aqua Real and Aqua Real Aquarium

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Aqua Real and Aqua Real Aquarium do not create accounts, contain advertising,
use analytics, or send information to a server operated by the developer.

## Location and weather

If the user grants location access, the Pebble phone companion obtains the
phone's current latitude and longitude. Coordinates rounded to four decimal
places are sent directly to `api.open-meteo.com` to request current
temperature, weather code, sunrise, and sunset. Coordinates are not sent to the
watch and are not stored by these projects. Open-Meteo may process and retain
request information according to its own
[Terms & Privacy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

The returned weather values and fetch time are cached in the Pebble phone
companion's local storage. Current weather values are also cached in persistent
storage on the watch. Watchface preferences are stored locally on the phone and
watch. This information is used only to render the aquarium.

## Retention and deletion

The developer does not receive or retain user data. Local data can be removed
by uninstalling the app/watchface and clearing its companion data through the
Pebble mobile app or operating-system app storage controls.

## Children, sale, and sharing

These projects do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
They do not sell personal information. Apart from the direct weather request
described above, they do not share information with third parties.

## Description (paste into the Emery asset collection)

Turn your Pebble Time 2 into a tiny living aquarium inspired by classic 3D aquarium screensavers. Seven dithered fish follow natural randomized paths, turn, dive, dart, change speed, and swim off-screen across three depth layers. A floating 3D clock offers smooth Pebble fonts, pixel glass, nautical stencil, and shimmering gold doubloons, with independent glyph motion, water refraction, and moving light.

Battery level becomes the waterline, with a buoy gauge riding the surface. Current weather and local sunrise/sunset shape clear, cloudy, foggy, rainy, snowy, and stormy skies across morning, noon, evening, and night. Animated caustics follow the photographed tank floor, while bubbles and a prepared 40-frame seaweed loop keep the scene alive.

Configure time/date sizes, 28 smooth-font choices, date formats and bold text, 12/24-hour behavior, doubloon brightness, and weather-float colors. Animation runs only while the backlight is on. Weather data: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Requires phone location permission for live weather; cached scenery works offline.

## Release notes

Version 1.7.0 makes extra-large smooth fonts substantially lighter by caching compact glyph row spans and reducing redundant depth passes. It also adds the separate Aqua Real Aquarium watch app release kit and refreshed store media. The watchface still animates only while the backlight is illuminated.
